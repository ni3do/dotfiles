Lighting

RippleEach keypress sends a Rosewater cream wave. Keys just behind the front dim briefly so the ripple reads even when the layer is already at full brightness.
Mods & Caps WordA held home-row mod lights that key and its mirror in rosewater cream. Caps Word pulses the letter keys in Mocha Yellow.
All keysEvery key lights in the active layer colour at full brightness (the board's 175 cap), including unmapped and transparent keys.
Status LEDsBoth halves match by colour: red = Nav (LED 1 and 4), green = Sym (LED 2 and 5), blue = Num (LED 3 and 6). All six light on Fn. All off on Base. Caps Word stays on the key LEDs only.
